# Angle: language

Find prose the diff adds that is longer, vaguer, or less standard than it needs to be, and name the replacement.

Guidelines on good language. Good prose is:

- boring: it uses industry standard terminology, literal naming, and no invented metaphors
- succinct and uses software-engineering style/vocabulary
- NO formality: Use plain english, tldr style
- NO mannered output: Plain, literal english
- NO storytelling: state the facts, not their history
- NO aphoristic text: Plain, literal english

Issues to flag:

- A number with no source: a threshold, count, percentage, or duration. Cite it or cut it.
- Field names, types, defaults, or flags copied from a model or parser. Point at the definition.
- Text about the text: what a section is, how it compares to its neighbours, why a rule is right. Sections instruct.
- Hedged directives: "worth a glance", "you might consider", "try to".
- The same point twice, adjacent or across files read together.
- An elevated verb for a plain act: "carry", "surface", "retire". Say what happened.
